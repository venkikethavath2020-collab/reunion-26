Welcome message

Welcome Back to Our Alma Mater

Years may have passed, but the memories we created at Telangana Government Tribal Welfare Residential Upgraded Junior College (TGTWURJC), Jinnaram remain close to our hearts.

From morning assemblies and classrooms to hostel life, sports, cultural events, examinations, and lifelong friendships, this campus shaped who we are today. The Grand Reunion 2026 is our opportunity to reconnect with classmates, honor our beloved teachers, revisit cherished memories, and create new ones together.

Once a TGTWURJC student, always a part of the TGTWURJC family.

Friendship. Gratitude. Home.

The ties that time cannot change.

About our school

Telangana Tribal Welfare Residential Upgraded Junior College

TGTWURJC, Jinnaram, Sangareddy District

Telangana Government Tribal Welfare Residential Upgraded Junior College has played an important role in nurturing generations of students through quality education, discipline, values, and character.

The institution has provided opportunities for academic excellence while fostering leadership, teamwork, respect, and lifelong friendships. For every student from the 2007-2012 batches, this campus represents unforgettable memories that continue to inspire us throughout life.

Today, we reunite not just as former classmates, but as one family connected forever by our alma mater.

School Administration Guidelines

A few shared commitments will help us celebrate our reunion with respect for the school, its students, and every guest.

Restricted Access

Alumni should remain within designated event areas unless accompanied by school representatives.

Respect School Property

Classrooms, laboratories, library, sports facilities, and infrastructure should not be used without prior permission.

Photography & Videography

Capture memories responsibly and follow the school’s privacy policies and committee instructions.

Appropriate Conduct

Maintain discipline, respectful language, and the dignity and values of the institution throughout the event.

No Political or Commercial Activities

This is a non-political, non-religious, and non-commercial event. Promotions, advertising, and marketing are prohibited.

No Public Criticism

Please do not publicly criticize school facilities or administration. Share constructive suggestions privately after the event.

Alcohol-Free Event

Participants under the influence of alcohol or carrying alcoholic beverages will not be permitted to attend.

Follow Safety Instructions

Follow all instructions issued by school authorities and volunteers, including emergency procedures and crowd management.

Keep the Campus Clean

Help maintain cleanliness throughout the event. Use dustbins and dispose of waste responsibly.

Parking & Traffic

Park vehicles only in designated parking areas and follow directions from volunteers and school authorities.

Food & Refreshments

Consume food only in designated areas while helping maintain cleanliness.
