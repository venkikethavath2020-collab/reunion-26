📜 Official Guidelines

School Administration Guidelines

These guidelines are issued by the school administration to ensure everyone enjoys a safe, respectful and memorable reunion.

Dear Alumni,

We warmly welcome you back to our beloved campus. Your cooperation and discipline will help us conduct this reunion successfully while respecting our institution, teachers, students and fellow alumni.

📍

Restricted Access

Stay only in the designated reunion areas unless accompanied by school representatives.

🏫

Respect School Property

Do not enter classrooms, laboratories, library or hostel areas without permission.

📸

Photography

Capture memories responsibly while respecting everyone's privacy.

🤝

Appropriate Conduct

Maintain discipline, respectful language and good behaviour throughout the event.

🚫

No Political Activities

Political, commercial and religious activities are strictly prohibited.

🍺

Alcohol-Free Event

Alcohol and intoxication are strictly prohibited inside the campus.

🛡️

Follow Instructions

Cooperate with volunteers and follow instructions from school authorities.

🗑️

Keep Campus Clean

Use dustbins and help us keep our beautiful campus clean.

🚗

Parking

Park only in designated parking areas and follow volunteer guidance.
